**Bio:**  
I was a PhD candidate at the department of computer science and engineering at Wright state university, where I worked in the area of knowledge representation and reasoning for the Semantic Web. After graduation, I joined Amazon as Software Development Engineer.

- *June, 2014 - August 2014* – Graduate Research Intern, [Siemens Corporate Research](http://www.siemens.com).
- *June, 2013 - August 2013* – Reaserch Intern, at [fluid Operations, Germany](http://www.fluidops.com).
- *March, 2008 - December 2010* – Worked as a Senior Software Engineer, at [Saba Software India Pvt Ltd.](http://saba.com).
- *May, 2006 - March 2008* – Worked as a Software Engineer at Infosys Technologies Ltd.
- *2002 - 2006* – Bachelors degree (BTech.) from [Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology.](http://www.daiict.ac.in/)
