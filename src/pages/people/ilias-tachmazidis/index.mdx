## Bio:

Ilias Tachmazidis is currently a PhD candidate at the  
[University of Huddersfield](http://www.hud.ac.uk/), UK, investigating whether, how, and to what extent large-scale reasoning can be achieved through mass parallelization.

He is supervised by  
[Prof. Grigoris Antoniou](http://www.hud.ac.uk/ourstaff/profile/index.php?staffuid=scomga2)  
and is currently a member of the  
[PARK (Planning, Autonomy and Representation of Knowledge)](http://www.hud.ac.uk/research/researchcentres/park/) research group.

He has received a **Best Paper Award** at RuleML 2013, followed by an **invited talk at AAAI-13**.

He graduated from the  
[University of Crete](http://www.csd.uoc.gr/en/), Greece, receiving both an **MSc (2012)** and a **BSc (2010)**.

His research interests include **Knowledge Representation and Reasoning**, **Semantic Web**, **High-Performance Computing**, and **Distributed Computing**.
