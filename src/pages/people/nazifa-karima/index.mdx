**Bio:**  
I am a graduate student at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Wright State University. I work under the supervision of [Dr. Pascal Hitzler](http://www.pascal-hitzler.de/) in the [Data Semantics Lab](http://dase.cs.wright.edu). I am currently involved with the Geolink project which is one of the may building blocks of the NSF-funded [Earthcube](http://earthcube.org/) program. The main aim of this project is to implement semantic web technologies to ensure improved data discovery, sharing integration and representation.
