# David Carral

## Bio:

Hey!

My name is David Carral and I was one of the PhD students working at DaSe Lab. My main interests are the study of Description Logic languages and the use of this knowledge representation languages to model information in different domains. I am now working at TU Dresden; you can find my new website [here](https://iccl.inf.tu-dresden.de/web/David_Carral).
