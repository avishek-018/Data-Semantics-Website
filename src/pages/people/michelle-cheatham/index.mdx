## Bio:

Bio: 
Hello! I'm Michelle Cheatham. I've had something of a winding career path -- after spending a decade working for various companies and government organizations, I decided to pursue a PhD. I graduated in the summer of 2014 and joined the faculty at Wright State just a month later. My research interests are in semantic data integration, automated population and structuring of knowledge bases, and privacy concerns related to Big Data.
