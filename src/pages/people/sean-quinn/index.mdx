## Bio

Sean graduated with a BSc. (Hons) in Computer Applications from [Dublin City University](https://www.dcu.ie/) in 2017 and was subsequently awarded an Irish Research Council scholarship to undertake a PhD in Artificial Intelligence at the [Insight Centre for Data Analytics](https://www.insight-centre.org), Dublin City University under the supervision of [Dr. Alessandra Mileo](https://www.computing.dcu.ie/~amileo/). Sean is a visiting research scholar at the Data Semantics Laboratory, Kansas State University for the fall semester 2019.

Sean’s research focuses on architecture-agnostic neural transfer and knowledge enhanced neural transfer.

## Research Interests

Neural Networks, Transfer learning, Representation Learning, Knowledge Representation
