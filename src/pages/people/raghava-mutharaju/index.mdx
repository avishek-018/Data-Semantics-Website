**Bio:**  
I am a PhD student in the Computer Science department of Wright State University. My advisor is [Prof. Pascal Hitzler](//daselab.cs.ksu.edu/people/pascal-hitzler). I also work closely with [Dr. Prabhaker Mateti](http://cecs.wright.edu/~pmateti/PM/index.html).

I am working on distributed approaches for OWL reasoning algorithms as well as distributed SPARQL querying processing over RDF data.

More details are available on my homepage: http://raghavam.github.io

**Research Interests:**  
Distributed OWL reasoning, Scalable SPARQL query processing, Knowledge Representation, Semantic Web applications
